About Me

An experienced Sound Designer and Acoustic Engineer with over 5 years of experience in interactive and immersive audio design. I specialize in creating sound systems for hardware, AR/VR, and non-traditional environments. I am skilled in applying Human-Centered Design and systems-thinking to product development, and I leverage tools like Wwise to build compelling and technically robust audio experiences.

New Work · Nov 2025

《ping-pong-ping-pong》

2025 Nuit Blanche Taipei 台北白晝之夜

Inspired by the native Taiwanese spatterdock (Nuphar shimadae), the project draws from its local name, which sounds identical to "Ping Pong." This phonetic link bridges botanical identity with the "Ping Pong Effect"—a spatial delay used in sound design.

In this interactive installation, when the audience strikes the spherical water tanks, it triggers a physical "Ping Pong" loop. The impact is captured and translated into acoustic vibrations, creating synchronized ripple patterns on the water's surface.

It is a complete sensory cycle, merging biological heritage, linguistic play, and technical audio systems into a single immersive experience.

  • Role: Sound Designer / Interactive Systems Engineer
  • Exhibition: Sidewalk opposite the Taipei Fine Arts Museum, Yuanshan, Taipei

Xiao & Dizi Lessons 笛・簫 線上教學 · Cours de xiao et de dizi

I teach xiao and dizi, the Chinese bamboo flutes, online with a focus on breath, tone, phrasing, ornamentation and repertoire. I have taught bamboo flute since 2002, in English and French. My background in sound design and acoustics helps me explain why a tone is airy or unstable, and how to fix it.

Beginners, wind players from other traditions, and fans of Chinese dramas, period games and East Asian culture are all welcome.

FAQ

Which language are the lessons in?

English or French. Lessons are online.

What time zone do you teach in?

I am in Taiwan (UTC+8) and can teach at almost any hour with advance booking. Tell me what works in your local time and we will arrange it week by week.

Do I need experience?

No. Lessons are adapted to your background and goals, whether you are a complete beginner or already play another wind instrument.

Where do I get a flute?

Good bamboo flutes are hard to find outside Asia and quality varies widely. I can choose one for you in Taiwan and ship it to you. We decide the key and budget together.

Do I need to read music?

No. We can work with numbered notation (jianpu), staff notation, or both.
