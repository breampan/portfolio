About Me

Interactive Installation · Nov 2025

《ping-pong-ping-pong》

2025 Nuit Blanche Taipei 台北白晝之夜

Inspired by the native Taiwanese spatterdock (Nuphar shimadae), the project draws from its local name, which sounds identical to "Ping Pong." This phonetic link bridges botanical identity with the "Ping Pong Effect"—a spatial delay used in sound design.

In this interactive installation, when the audience strikes the spherical water tanks, it triggers a physical "Ping Pong" loop. The impact is captured and translated into acoustic vibrations, creating synchronized ripple patterns on the water's surface.

It is a complete sensory cycle, merging biological heritage, linguistic play, and technical audio systems into a single immersive experience.

  • Role: Sound Designer / Interactive Systems Engineer
  • Exhibition: Sidewalk opposite the Taipei Fine Arts Museum, Yuanshan, Taipei

Time-Layered Soundscape Map ｜山城流域時光聲景地圖

Web Prototype & Field Recording | Ongoing

Before a new expressway opens, the sound of the Dajia River valley between Dongshi and Shigang is about to change for good. The old railway, the waterways buried under roads and the voices of the elders now live mostly in memory. This map is my attempt to keep what is disappearing: to record it while it can still be heard, and to put each sound back in the place where it belongs.

I want these sounds to keep their original meaning, as part of how people lived here rather than material to be sampled. So the map stays under fog until you walk or ride there yourself, and the voices of the people who live here are shared only after they have listened back and agreed. The video shows the ride along the Dongfeng greenway towards Shigang, beside the new expressway. This is a prototype, and it will keep growing.

My Seasonal Imprints ｜我的四季印記

Co-Creator, Sound Design | 2024

Public art piece made with artist Syu Hong-Hui and about 60 students over two months and four sessions, shown at the General Education Expo at National Formosa University (Dec 2024). Syu taught plaster plant printing and I taught sound design. Students cast campus plants in plaster; I edited their field recordings into sound clips and built a sensor-triggered playback system, with contact speakers behind the plaster panels, set inside a wall of reclaimed window frames. Syu and I collected the old windows from Tainan and Taichung and designed the wooden wall frame together. We also turned old windows into planter boxes to bring a disused sink back to life, with a solar-powered automatic misting system I installed. Thanks to the friends who helped us build it and lent the power tools.

Dialogue in Silence

Multichannel Sound Creation | 2024

A choreographic work for the dome, nominated for the 2024 FullDome Festival Jena (New Talent). I created the multichannel sound, which grew out of Étude_00, a spatial composition from a year-long IRCAM × C-LAB workshop in 2022: sorting sounds by six attributes (texture, concrete, realistic, instrumental, electronic, cyclical), then recomposing and interweaving them. A dancer who heard the piece at a concert at the Sound Art Center of Taiwan's National University of the Arts asked to use it as the score for a dome dance work. Made together with Chih-Jung Hsu, Chia-Yi Shih and Chia-Hsiang Lee.

Feel Together — Taiwan Sound Lab / C-LAB

Control Interface Design & Sound-to-Vibration Translation | 2023–2025

A cultural-equity project of the Taoyuan Bureau of Cultural Affairs, ITRI and C-LAB (Taiwan Contemporary Culture Lab), made with the Deaf theatre company Duck Studio and neuroscientist Chen-Chung Lee: a vest that lets people feel music through the body. Workshops with the company reshaped the project, and they proposed the balloon method we explored together, one person speaking or singing against a balloon while the other listens with an ear on it.

My part: the sound-to-vibration mapping in Max for Live, a control interface that drives several vibration motors with different patterns at once, and live operation. The project ended in a performance where Deaf dancers improvised from the vibrations while the audience wore vests, and toured to the National Theater & Concert Hall, the Taoyuan Iron Rose Music Festival and the National Museum of Taiwan Literature.

French Railway (SNCF)

Audio Branding & Alert-Tone Generator | Internship, 2020

The goal was an evacuation alarm that carries the brand: alert sounds that fit the spirit of the company rather than a generic siren. I designed the alert tones and built a parametric alert-tone generator in Max for Live and Ableton. The method rested on listening tests, a sound moodboard made with IRCAM SpeAK, and research on alarm sounds and brand expectations.

Co-authored with Guillaume Lemaitre and Claire Chaufour and presented at WCRR 2022 (Going Home Safely). The tones are in service on the rail network of the Paris region (Île-de-France).

Xiao & Dizi Lessons 笛・簫 線上教學 · Cours de xiao et de dizi

I teach xiao and dizi, the Chinese bamboo flutes, online with a focus on breath, tone, phrasing, ornamentation and repertoire. I have taught bamboo flute since 2002, in English and French. My background in sound design and acoustics helps me explain why a tone is airy or unstable, and how to fix it.

Beginners, wind players from other traditions, and fans of Chinese dramas, period games and East Asian culture are all welcome.

FAQ

Which language are the lessons in?

English or French. Lessons are online.

What time zone do you teach in?

I am in Taiwan (UTC+8) and can teach at almost any hour with advance booking. Tell me what works in your local time and we will arrange it week by week.

Do I need experience?

No. Lessons are adapted to your background and goals, whether you are a complete beginner or already play another wind instrument.

Where do I get a flute?

Good bamboo flutes are hard to find outside Asia and quality varies widely. I can choose one for you in Taiwan and ship it to you. We decide the key and budget together.

Do I need to read music?

No. We can work with numbered notation (jianpu), staff notation, or both.
